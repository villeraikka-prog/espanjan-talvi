**Fit and current route:** Its public “Buscamos Bloggers” page invites Torrevieja-related articles, says the team must accept the piece, and says it will place a link to the contributor's website or social profile at the end. It directs applicants to its contact form. The call does not state whether publication is free, what languages are accepted, or whether the link can point to the relevant profile page. It mentions a preference for writing skill and some English, and the site exposes several language versions. No named editor or direct editorial email was found.
**Opportunity:** conditional qualification inquiry only. Do not write the full article until they confirm the language, fee, and link terms.
**Contact:** https://torrevieja.com/contacto/
**Opportunity page:** https://torrevieja.com/colabora-con-nosotros/
**Proposed language:** Spanish for the first inquiry because the public call is in Spanish. Ask whether a Finnish-language version, Spanish version, or translation is appropriate for their Finnish audience.
**Target URL:** https://espanjan-talvi.netlify.app/kohteet/torrevieja/
**Reader value:** a practical, source-aware way for Finnish winter visitors to compare the character and everyday implications of Torrevieja's central, La Mata, and coastal areas before choosing a long-stay address. Avoid unsupported claims about current opening hours, exact travel times, or rental prices.
**Editorial angle/title:** “Torrevieja en invierno: tres preguntas para elegir la zona de una estancia larga” (“Torrevieja talvella: kolme kysymystä pidempään oleskeluun sopivan alueen valintaan”).

1. Why a long winter stay makes the exact address matter.
2. Compare town-centre, La Mata, and coastal/residential settings by everyday access, not by a single “best area” claim.
3. A pre-booking checklist: check the exact address, nearby grocery/pharmacy, public transport route, and seasonal opening information from current sources.
4. Close with a short transparent author/site note and one contextual link to the Torrevieja profile, only if allowed.

**Natural link position:** a short author/resource note at the end, pointing to the Finnish Torrevieja profile. The publisher's public call says it places a link at the end, but permission to choose this exact deep URL still needs confirmation.
