# Backlink acquisition tracker

This is the working register for individual, relevant backlink prospects. A contact route or an invitation to submit content is **not** a confirmed backlink. Mark an opportunity “free and viable” only after the publisher confirms its terms. Do not submit, publish, pay, exchange links, or represent the site as a business listing without explicit owner approval.

## Reusable operating sequence

1. Review the existing opportunity research and map one reader-relevant target page.
2. Verify the prospect's current public submission rules and contact route. Record source URLs and date.
3. Qualify topic fit, eligibility, fee, link policy, language, disclosure, and whether the publication is public/indexable. Do not infer “free” from an invitation that omits a price.
4. Prepare a short tailored inquiry first when any condition is unknown. Prepare a full original article only after the editor confirms a suitable free route and submission requirements.
5. Keep outreach as a draft. A human approves and sends it; Codex must not contact the prospect without that approval.
6. Record response and next action. If the proposal needs a payment, reciprocal link, misleading registration, mass submission, or unnatural anchor, close it as rejected.
7. Record a backlink as acquired only after its public URL is live. Verify the page, destination URL, context, and link behavior; record the verification date and any uncertainty. Never infer `rel` attributes from a search snippet.

This tracker is intentionally a Markdown file, not a publishing database. It contains no credentials or private account data.
